Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026. DRAFT — have counsel review before launch.

What we collect

Account data (work email, workspace ID), the API key hash, billing identifiers from Stripe, and usage counts (workflow runs, agent steps, tokens, sandbox minutes). We do not store card numbers; Stripe handles payment details.

Content you submit

Task prompts, repository contents and agent outputs are processed to run your workflows. To generate plans and code, relevant content is sent to our AI model provider (Anthropic) and, if vector memory is enabled, to our embeddings provider. Execution logs and an audit trail are retained for your workspace.

How we use it

To operate and secure the service, meter and bill usage, prevent abuse, and communicate with you about your account. We do not sell personal data.

Processors

Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI models), and our hosting and database providers act as processors on our behalf.

Retention and deletion

We keep workspace data while your account is active. Unpaid signups are deleted automatically after 48 hours. To request access or deletion of your data, contact us below.
